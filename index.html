      Flights

      British Airways flies nonstop from Heathrow to both Nashville and Austin, so the London lads can fly into one city and home from the other instead of doubling back. Airlines usually release seats about 11 months ahead.

      Getting around

      • Ubers and walking in both cities. Downtown Nashville is very walkable.
      • Hire a car for day trips: Jack Daniel’s is about 1½ hours away, the nearest casino about 2½.
      • Rideshares can’t drop off at the F1 circuit on race weekend, so we’d use the official shuttles or a parking pass.

      Before we go

      • UK passports need an ESTA. It costs $40.27 on the official US government site. Ignore the middleman sites that charge more.
      • Check passports are valid for the whole trip.
      • Travel insurance, ideally with cover for F1 tickets.

      When to book

      • Austin hotels for F1 weekend: as early as possible. They sell out and climb.
      • Flights: from late 2026, when October 2027 seats appear.
      • Football: once the SEC and NFL fixtures are out in 2027.
